      2                                Sales Staff                                   Manages customer information, receives consultation requests, advises customers, creates and manages vehicle sales            Monitors transactions, supports vehicle import and export operations, updates vehicle status, checks operational information, and coordinates vehicle handover. Use Case Diagram                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          b. Use Case Descriptions                    The following use cases describe the main interactions between actors and the Electric Car Sales Management System. Each use case represents a specific business function within the system.                                                                    No.                                      Use Case                                      Primary Actor                                      Description                                          UC-01                              Register Account                              Customer                              Allows a customer to create a new account by providing personal information such as full name, phone number, email, and password. The system uses OTP verification to confirm the registration.                                     UC-02                              Login Account                              Customer, Employee, Admin                              Allows users to authenticate and access the system according to their assigned role and permissions.                                     UC-03                              Verify OTP                              Customer                              Allows the customer to verify registration or important transaction information using a valid OTP sent by the system.                                     UC-04                              Search Vehicles                              Customer                              Allows customers to search and filter electric vehicles by brand, model, price range, version, color, and technical specifications.                                     UC-05                              View Vehicle Details                              Customer                              Allows customers to view detailed vehicle information including images, price, specifications, promotions, payment policies, and vehicle status.                                     UC-06                              Consult Vehicle                              Customer, Sales Staff                              Allows customers to submit a consultation request for a selected vehicle and allows sales staff to receive and handle the request.                                     UC-07                              Get AI Product Consultation                              Customer, AI Assistant                              Allows customers to ask questions, compare vehicle models or versions, analyze their needs, and receive vehicle recommendations based on factors such as budget and intended use.                                     UC-08                              Create Sales Transaction                              Sales Staff                              Allows sales staff to create a vehicle sales transaction containing customer information, selected vehicle, selling price, payment method, responsible employee, and transaction status.                                     UC-09                              Manage Vehicle Inventory                              Admin, Operations Officer, Manager                              Allows authorized employees to manage vehicle information, inventory records, vehicle locations, import/export activities, and vehicle status.                                     UC-10                              Process Deposit                              Sales Staff, Accountant                              Allows the system to record and process a customer's vehicle deposit, update the transaction, and change the vehicle status accordingly.                                     UC-11                              Process Payment                              Accountant                              Allows the accountant to record, verify, and manage customer payments, including the 30% deposit and the 70% remaining balance (paid directly by the customer for full payment or disbursed by the financial partner/bank for installment purchases), and track the order's outstanding debt.                                      UC-12                              View Financial Partner Information                               Accountant                              Allows the accountant to manage installment plans, down payments, loan information, payment terms, interest, payment schedules, payment history, and outstanding debt.Allow customers and sales staff to view the list of partner banks/financial institutions and reference interest rates; when a customer selects installment payment, the system will only record the financial partner on the order.                                     UC-13                              Manage Contract and Invoice                              Sales Staff, Accountant                              Allows authorized staff to prepare and manage vehicle sales contracts and invoices after the required payment and transaction conditions are completed.
